CCP Games, 2014

EVE Online runs one universe, cut into solar systems.

The partition key you dream about: over 7,500 keys, each its own sealed world, each on a node.

On 27 January 2014 the sovereignty bill came due, Pandemic Legion's holding corp had left auto-pay unchecked, and B-R5RB — a dead-end system, and Pandemic Legion's staging system — fell open to capture. Both coalitions came: 6,058 pilots inside that one system, 2,670 of them at once.

One key, one node. CCP's standing answer to a fight it knows about is reinforced hardware — but players request that in advance, and it "only reinforces the actual solar system that is requested". You have to know which key will burn before it burns.

What was left was Time Dilation: slow the clock inside the overloaded system until its queue drains. CCP Veritas had written it up in 2011 with the ceiling stated up front — "Time dilation does not solve everything." B-R5RB sat on the floor of it, 10% of normal time, for 21 hours: "just over 2 hours worth of simulation", a slideshow inside, normal outside. 75 titans died, 11 trillion ISK, on CCP's own conversion $300,000-$330,000.

Splitting the key was never on the menu. Everything in a solar system has to see everything else, in order; cut it across two machines and you do not get a faster fight, you get no fight.

One key, one machine, and the key cannot be cut. What would you do? Write it before you read on.
