A researcher at DEC's Systems Research Center wrote up an algorithm for getting a group of unreliable computers to agree on a single value. He framed the paper as the archaeology of a parliament on a fictional Greek island. Reviewers did not find it funny, and it sat unpublished for years.

The problem it solved has not moved since: a machine that has been granted something cannot know, at the moment it acts, whether it still holds it. 1998
