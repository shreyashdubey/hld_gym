THE METHOD · BY HAND, TODAY03 / 09

How I extract a person.

what I find out, and how

  • What they already knowprior-knowledge map · diagnostic pretest with confidence ratings
  • How they thinkthink-aloud protocol on one real problem · cognitive task analysis
  • How they learn fastlearning-history interview: what stuck, what did not, why
  • How the knowledge has to come outtransfer-appropriate practice: whiteboard, bench and code, or papers and math
  • What makes it stick for themjudgments of learning, then a delayed recall check: calibration

what the book does with it

  • retrieval practice: every chapter ends by asking, not telling
  • spacing and interleaving: chapters return, mixed
  • worked examples that fade into problems
  • dual coding: every mechanism drawn and said
  • elaborative interrogation: the chapter keeps asking why
  • concrete before abstract: a story, then the mechanism, then the math
  • desirable difficulty: the lock

Manual: the interviews, the profile, the chapter order, the verification. Automated: the compile, the lock, the grader, the reels. Every conversation adds a rule the method did not have.
