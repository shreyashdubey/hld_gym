THE METHOD · BY HAND, TODAY02 / 10

How I extract a person.

what I find out, and how

  • What they already knowprior-knowledge map · diagnostic pretest with confidence ratings
  • How they thinkthink-aloud protocol on one real problem · cognitive task analysis
  • How they learn fastlearning-history interview: what stuck, what did not, why
  • How the knowledge has to come outtransfer-appropriate practice: whiteboard, bench and code, or papers and math
  • What makes it stick for themjudgments of learning, then a delayed recall check: calibration

what the book does with it

  • retrieval practice: every chapter ends by asking, not telling
  • spacing and interleaving: chapters return, mixed
  • worked examples that fade into problems
  • dual coding: every mechanism drawn and said
  • elaborative interrogation: the chapter keeps asking why
  • concrete before abstract: a story, then the mechanism, then the math
  • desirable difficulty: the lock

Manual: the interviews, the profile, the chapter order, the verification. Automated: the compile, the lock, the grader, the reels. Every conversation adds a rule the method did not have.

SHREYASH · P2C02 · IDEMPOTENCY, EXACTLY-ONCE, AND LEDGERS · you tapped pay, the signal died, you were not charged twice05 / 10
p2c02 · idempotency, exactly-once, and ledgersquestion · origin · watch · lock · rebuild · grade · probe · reveal

You tapped Pay. Your signal died. The app retried.

Why weren't you charged twice?

origin · dalmellington, 2015 · horizon online, fujitsu / post office
The books balanced. The books were wrong.

A subpostmistress scanned one £8,000 pouch once. Horizon recorded it four times. The retry was honest: a script thought it had not finished and repeated its last step, and nothing remembered that the first write had happened. 112 occurrences, 88 branches, five years. People were prosecuted over ledgers that agreed with themselves.

who got hurt, and what nobody had remembered. now the mechanism.
The key travels with the request. The client minted it, once. The key is stored with the result, in the same transaction as the effect. The money moved exactly once. Now the response has to make it back. The signal died here. The client never saw the OK. The client retries with the same key. That is the whole trick. Same key: replay the response, not the work.
That's it. It's gone.
